About Véloran

Véloran is clothing with a quiet orbit. The brand is built around a single mark, a four-point star held in orbit, and every piece follows the same rule: clean cuts, dark tones, and nothing louder than it needs to be.

The collection is small on purpose. Right now that means sweat sets and tees, released as pre-orders so each piece is made with intent rather than stacked on a shelf.

Questions, ideas or press? Write to or message us on WhatsApp.

Help

How do I pre-order?

Open a piece, choose your size and quantity, and add it to your pre-order. Review your cart, then check out with your contact and delivery details, a payment method, and a final review. When you place the pre-order it is saved with us straight away and you get a reference number. You can also send us a copy by email or WhatsApp, but that is optional.

What happens after I place a pre-order?

We reply by email to confirm your pre-order, shipping and taxes before you pay anything.

Which payment methods can I choose?

Bank transfer (we email you the account details) or card via a secure payment link we send you. Never type card numbers on this site.

The email did not open. What now?

After placing your pre-order, tap "Copy order details" and paste them into an email to . Your pre-order is already saved, the email copy is optional.

Do I need an account?

No. You can pre-order as a guest. Signing up with Google or an email link saves your name and email so checkout is filled in for you.

Sizing, shipping and returns

Sizes run S to XL. Shipping, taxes, returns and sizing advice are confirmed by email when your pre-order is processed. If you are unsure about a size, email us before ordering.

Still stuck? Email or message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026. This explains what Véloran collects when you browse, sign up or pre-order, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you sign up: your name, your email, and, if you sign up by email, your confirmation that you are 18 or older. Signing in with Google gives us the name and email on your Google account. When you pre-order: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, the pieces and sizes you chose, and your payment method choice. We never ask for card numbers on this site.

How we use it

To process and deliver your pre-order, to contact you about it, and, if you signed up, to email you about the launch. We do not sell your data.

How your details reach us

When you place a pre-order, your order details are saved in our order database (Google Firebase, Cloud Firestore). They can be read only by Véloran admins, and by you if you ordered while signed in. If you sign up, your name and email are kept with Firebase Authentication and in the same database. You sign in with Google or with a one-time sign-in link sent to your email. You can also send us an optional copy of your order by email or WhatsApp.

What is saved on your device

If you sign up, your name and email are saved in your browser on that device so checkout can be filled in for you, Firebase keeps you signed in on that device, and a small flag remembers that you closed the sign-up pop-up. Clearing your browser data removes both.

Third parties

This page loads fonts from Google Fonts, Firebase code from Google, and a 3D library from a public content network, which means those services can see your IP address when the page loads. Sign-in, sign-up and order details are processed by Google Firebase. If you contact us through Instagram or WhatsApp, their own privacy policies apply. We use no advertising trackers on this page. We share your details only as needed to deliver and take payment for your order.

Age

Pre-orders and sign-ups are for people aged 18 or older.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to stop launch emails, by writing to . We keep order details only as long as needed to fulfil your order and meet legal obligations.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
